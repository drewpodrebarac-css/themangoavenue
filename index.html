The name

There really is a mango tree

A massive mango tree grows right above our shop, and it drops dozens of mangoes every single day. We named the place after it.

You'll find us where the tree is: the first shop on your right as you walk into El Tunco — surf gear in front, coffee in the morning, a glass of wine as the sun goes down over Surf City El Salvador.
