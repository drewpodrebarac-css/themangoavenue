Know before you paddle

The waves around us

This coast is a surfer's dream — perfect rights everywhere, and the water stays warm 365 days a year. Six breaks within 25 minutes of the shop, and we'll take you to any of them: boards, lessons and a local guide, any day of the year.

Beginners

El Tunco Beach

Sandy-bottom rollers right in front of town — where our first-timer lessons happen. Two minutes' walk from the shop.

Live Surfline forecast ↗
Intermediate +

La Bocana

El Tunco's rivermouth peak: fast, punchy lefts and rights over rock — the area's only true left. A 45-second speed walk from our door 😄

Live Surfline forecast ↗
All levels

El Sunzal

A long, friendly right-hand point that rolls forever — longboard heaven and the best wave in the country to progress on. Ten minutes' walk up the beach.

Advanced

Punta Roca

El Salvador's world-famous right point in La Libertad — fast, hollow, world-championship pedigree. Fifteen minutes by car.

The name

There really is a mango tree

A massive mango tree grows right above our shop, and it drops dozens of mangoes every single day. We named the place after it.

You'll find us where the tree is: the first shop on your right as you walk into El Tunco — surf gear in front, coffee in the morning, a glass of wine as the sun goes down over Surf City El Salvador.
